Structure exploited: the text slice has both whole-message neighborhoods and repeated short phrases. Parent A supplied the global BWT/MTF transform and bijective-binary coding of zero-rank runs; Parent B supplied exclusion-based sparse order-4 PPM with a globally valid order-0 floor. They reinforce one another by modeling the transformed RUNA/RUNB/literal stream with one synchronized PPM state, rather than paying for separate event models. The parents do not require a conflict resolution: the lower-order exclusion rule is retained and the flat histogram is replaced by the PPM combination.

The artifact was written independently from the scorer contract. It stores the original length and, only for the transformed candidate, the BWT primary row; the high bit of the length selects the candidate without adding a mode byte. The transformed candidate uses an explicit EOF token, and all decoder state is derived only from the payload.
